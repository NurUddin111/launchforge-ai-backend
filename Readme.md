# 💻 Project Overview

LaunchForge AI is an AI-powered startup generation platform that transforms a simple business idea into a complete startup blueprint.

Users can authenticate, generate startup ideas with AI, save them to the database, and manage all generated startups from their dashboard.

---

# 🎯 Problem Statement

Entrepreneurs often spend weeks validating ideas, creating business plans, selecting technology, planning MVPs, and preparing go-to-market strategies.

This process is time-consuming and requires significant business knowledge.

---

# 💡 Solution

LaunchForge AI leverages Google Gemini AI to instantly generate:

* Startup Summary
* Problem Statement
* Solution
* Business Model
* MVP Features
* Tech Stack
* Marketing Strategy
* Roadmap
* Estimated Budget

Everything is stored in PostgreSQL and can be managed through a personalized dashboard.
